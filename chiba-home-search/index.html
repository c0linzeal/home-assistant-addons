    💡 Tips for buying as a non-Japanese speaker

    • Translate the page: In Chrome, right-click anywhere → “Translate to English”. On phones, use the Google app or your browser's built-in translate.
    • Prices are in 万円 (man-en = ×10,000 yen). “3,980万円” means ¥39,800,000. This tool shows you the figure to look for.
    • Foreigners can buy property in Japan with no residency or citizenship requirement. Financing (a mortgage from a Japanese bank) is the harder part and usually needs residency + stable income.
    • Budget extra ~6–8% on top of the price for taxes, agent fee, and registration costs.
    • An English-speaking agent can pull the same listings and handle paperwork. Use these searches to find homes you like, then send the listing link to an agent.
